Inventory sharing

You control access to your inventory. Each invitation is one-way, and access begins only after the recipient accepts.

Shared inventory is read-only in this release, including grants marked Read and write. Write access will be enabled in a later development step.

Invite by email

Armory Ledger sends the invitation through this installation’s configured email. If the recipient has not used this installation yet, the invitation waits until they sign in with this email address. The email contains no inventory records.
